How it works

From words to one well-chosen emoji.

01

Clean & tokenize

The message is lowercased and split the way the training tweets were: URLs, mentions, hashtags, emoticons, and words.

02

Read both directions

Each token becomes an embedding. A bidirectional LSTM reads the message forward and backward to capture context.

03

Pick the best fit

A final layer scores 43 emoji classes. The highest probability wins; the next two are shown as alternatives.

The exported embedding table uses per-row int8 quantization, shrinking the model from 122 MB to about 31 MB while keeping predictions aligned with the original PyTorch model.
